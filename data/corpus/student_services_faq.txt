1. GENERAL OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document outlines the official policies, student support services, registration
guidelines, fee refund procedures, and document authentication protocols for students
enrolled at the Faculty of Economics and Political Science (FEPS), Cairo University
(جامعة القاهرة - كلية الاقتصاد والعلوم السياسية).

This guide complements the official FEPS Student Handbook (Credit Hours System) and
serves as a primary reference for administrative inquiries, campus resources, and
student affairs protocols.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION, TRANSCRIPTS, AND CERTIFICATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Office of Student Records and Document Verification
All requests regarding official transcripts, enrollment verification certificates,
graduation certificates, and educational authentication are handled exclusively by:
- Responsible Unit: Office of Student Records and Document Verification (إدارة شؤون الطلاب والسجلات)
- Office Location: Main Administration Building, Ground Floor, Room 104
- Office Hours: Sunday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 2:30 PM (Closed Fridays, Saturdays, and official public holidays)
- Official Inquiries Email: records-verification@feps.edu.eg

2.2 Document Processing Times and Fees
- Official Academic Transcript (English or Arabic): Issued within 3 to 5 business days upon submission of the request form. Standard fee: 100 EGP per copy.
- Enrollment Verification Certificate (شهادة قيد): Issued within 2 business days. Standard fee: 50 EGP per copy.
- Degree Completion Certificate (Temporary / Provisional): Issued within 5 to 7 business days following official faculty board approval. Standard fee: 150 EGP.
- Urgent Processing Service: Available for transcripts and enrollment certificates for an additional expediting fee of 100 EGP (issued within 24 business hours).

2.3 Document Authentication and Attestation
Students requiring external authentication (Ministry of Higher Education or Ministry of
Foreign Affairs attestation) must first obtain an authorized faculty stamp and dean's
signature from Room 104 before submitting documents to the Central University Administration
at Cairo University Main Campus.

3. TUITION AND SERVICE FEE REFUND POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Tuition and Course Fee Refund Schedule
Students who officially withdraw from courses or from the semester are eligible for a
refund according to the following strict timeline:
- Prior to the official first day of classes: 100% refund of paid tuition and lab fees, minus a non-refundable administrative registration fee of 200 EGP.
- During the first week of classes (Week 1): 75% refund of paid tuition fees.
- During the second week of classes (Week 2): 50% refund of paid tuition fees.
- After the end of the second week of classes (Day 15 onward): 0% refund (strictly no refunds granted under any circumstances).

3.2 Crucial Exception Regarding Application and Processing Fees
Refund rules apply solely to course tuition and refundable lab fees. Service fees,
including transcript processing fees, admission application fees, credential evaluation
fees, and locker rental administrative fees, are strictly non-refundable once document
or application processing has officially begun. If an applicant cancels an administrative
request before processing has commenced, a 50% refund may be requested in writing.

3.3 Refund Application Procedure
To submit a formal fee refund request:
1. Complete the Student Refund Application Form (Form REF-01), downloadable from the student portal or obtainable from the Finance Office.
2. Attach the original official university bank receipt or electronic payment voucher proving payment.
3. Attach written approval of course drop or formal semester withdrawal signed by the Academic Advisor and the Vice Dean for Education and Student Affairs.
4. Submit the complete application dossier to the Student Financial Services Office (Building B, Room 102).
5. Approved refunds are disbursed via bank transfer to the student's registered bank account or via a university treasury voucher within 15 to 20 business days.

4. THIRD-PARTY REPRESENTATION AND POWER OF ATTORNEY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 General Rule on In-Person Submission
Under university privacy and educational record security regulations, students are
expected to complete all administrative procedures, course registrations, and document
pickups in person.

4.2 Third-Party Representation Guidelines
A representative (parent, sibling, or designated agent) may act on behalf of an enrolled
student only under the following explicit conditions:
- Formal Authorization by Power of Attorney: The representative must present an official, validly notarized General or Special Power of Attorney (توكيل رسمي موثق من مصلحة الشهر العقاري) authorizing academic and administrative transactions.
- Immediate Family Proxy for Non-Sensitive Tasks: For routine transactions only (submitting documents or paying fees, but excluding grade appeals or graduation certificate pickup), a first-degree relative (parent or sibling) may be authorized by submitting:
  (a) A signed Student Proxy Authorization Letter specifying the designated representative and purpose;
  (b) A photocopy of the student's valid National ID or Passport and Student ID;
  (c) The original and a photocopy of the representative's valid National ID or Passport;
  (d) Official proof of family relation (e.g., family civil registry or birth certificate).
- Strictly In-Person Transactions: Official final degree certificates, graduation diplomas, and disciplinary hearings require the student's physical in-person presence or an explicit, specific legal power of attorney mentioning the document by title.

5. ACADEMIC ADVISING AND COURSE REGISTRATION (CREDIT HOURS SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Role of Academic Advisors
Every student admitted to FEPS is assigned a faculty member as an Academic Advisor (المرشد الأكاديمي).
The advisor's role is to guide course selection, monitor cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA),
ensure prerequisite compliance, and authorize registration forms each semester.

5.2 Credit Hour Load Limits
- Minimum Semester Load: 12 credit hours per regular semester (Fall or Spring).
- Standard Semester Load: 15 to 18 credit hours.
- Maximum Overload (Dean's List / High Academic Standing): Up to 21 credit hours, permissible only for students with a cumulative GPA of 3.50 or higher and subject to advisor and department chair approval.
- Academic Probation Load: Students on academic warning (cumulative GPA below 2.00) are restricted to a maximum load of 12 credit hours per semester.
- Summer Term Maximum Load: 6 credit hours (or 7 credit hours if one course is a 4-credit course or graduation-dependent).

5.3 Add / Drop Period and Course Withdrawal
- Add/Drop Window: The official add/drop period extends through the first two weeks of the Fall and Spring semesters. Courses dropped during this period do not appear on the student's academic transcript and incur no academic penalty.
- Formal Course Withdrawal ('W' Grade): Students may withdraw from a course up to the end of the 8th week of the semester with the approval of the course instructor and academic advisor. The course appears on the transcript with a grade of 'W' (Withdrawn), which carries zero grade points and does not affect the GPA.
- Withdrawal after Week 8 is prohibited except under certified medical emergency approved by the Faculty Medical Board; unauthorized stoppage after Week 8 results in an administrative failure grade of 'F'.

6. STUDENT IDENTIFICATION CARDS AND CAMPUS SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Student ID Card Issuance and Replacement
- Primary Issuance: Issued free of charge to all newly matriculated students during orientation week upon submission of enrollment paperwork.
- Lost ID Card Replacement Procedure:
  1. File a lost item notification at the Campus Security Office (Building A gate).
  2. Pay the replacement fee of 150 EGP at the University Treasury.
  3. Submit the payment receipt, a copy of National ID/Passport, and two recent passport-style photographs to the Student Affairs Office (Room 101).
  4. The replacement card is printed and ready for pickup within 48 business hours.

6.2 Campus Health Center and Medical Insurance
All enrolled FEPS students are entitled to comprehensive primary medical care, emergency
treatment, and subsidized pharmaceuticals at the Cairo University Student Hospital (مستشفى الطلبة - جامعة القاهرة)
located in Giza.
- To access medical services, students must present a validated FEPS Student ID card for the current academic term.
- Medical Excuse Validation: Any medical absence from examinations or mandatory lectures must be certified by the Cairo University Student Hospital within 48 hours of the onset of illness. Private clinic certificates are not accepted unless authenticated and countersigned by the University Medical Board.

6.3 Library Services and Regulations
- Central Faculty Library Location: 3rd and 4th Floors, FEPS Main Building.
- Working Hours: Sunday to Thursday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM; Saturday, 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
- Undergraduate Borrowing Privileges: Up to 3 books for a loan period of 14 calendar days. Books may be renewed once for an additional 7 days if no hold has been placed by another borrower.
- Overdue Fines: 5 EGP per book per business day of delay.
- Reference materials, rare manuscripts, and periodicals are reserved for in-library reading only.

6.4 Campus Locker Rentals
Lockers are available for students in the lower level of Building A.
- Annual rental fee: 200 EGP per academic year (non-refundable).
- Key security deposit: 100 EGP (fully refundable upon return of the locker key at the conclusion of the academic year).
- Application period: First three weeks of the Fall semester at the Student Activities Office.

7. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY, ATTENDANCE, AND GRADE APPEALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Attendance Regulations
Class attendance is mandatory in all lectures, discussion sections, and computer laboratories.
- Minimum Required Attendance: 75% of scheduled contact hours.
- Deprivation of Examination Right: If a student's unexcused absences exceed 25% of total course sessions, the faculty council issues a formal deprivation notice (حرمان من الامتحان), barring the student from sitting for the final examination and assigning a failing grade of 'F'.

7.2 Final Grade Appeal Procedure
Students who identify a material discrepancy or grading calculation error on their official
final semester results may file a formal grade contestation:
1. Contestation Window: Appeals must be filed within 10 calendar days following the official publication of semester results on the online student portal.
2. Contestation Fee: An administrative appeal fee of 100 EGP per course must be deposited at the University Treasury.
3. Review Process: The Academic Appeals Committee convenes to recheck the arithmetic addition of marks and verify that all examination booklet sections were evaluated.
4. Fee Refund on Error Correction: If the appeal reveals a grading error that results in an upward grade modification, the 100 EGP fee is refunded in full to the student.
5. Finality: The verdict of the Faculty Review Board is final and conclusive.
